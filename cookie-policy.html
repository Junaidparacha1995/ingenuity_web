Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies help Ingenuity Systems keep the site secure, remember useful preferences, and understand what needs improvement.

Last updated September 21, 2026
On this pageWhat cookies areTypes we useYour controlsChanges

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar browser storage values placed on a device when a website is visited. They can help a site remember a session, protect forms, measure performance, or keep preferences consistent.

2. Types of technologies we may use

Essential technologies

These support core functions such as security, routing, session handling, accessibility, and the basic operation of forms or interactive experiences. They generally cannot be switched off through site preferences.

Preference technologies

These help remember choices such as interface state, display preferences, or whether a visitor has already seen a particular prompt. If they are disabled, some conveniences may not persist between visits.

Measurement and performance

Where enabled, limited technical information may help us understand page performance, navigation patterns, and broken experiences. We use that insight to improve site structure and content rather than to sell personal information.

Third-party technologies

Embedded services, hosted fonts, analytics, communication tools, or external media may set their own cookies or similar technologies. Their use is controlled by the relevant provider’s policy.

We aim to keep optional tracking proportionate to the value it provides. We do not use cookies to collect passwords, private keys, or API credentials.

3. Your controls

You can usually review, block, delete, or limit cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential technologies may affect security, navigation, forms, or other site functions. Browser settings vary, so check the help documentation for your browser or device.

4. Related information

For more detail about personal information, see our Privacy Policy. For the general rules for using the site, see our Terms & Conditions.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our site, providers, or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be available on this page with a revised date.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy can be sent to info@ingenuitysys.com.

Cookie behavior can vary by browser, device, region, and the services active at a particular time.
