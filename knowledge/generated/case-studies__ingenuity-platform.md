 Internal Product Case Study
 From marketing website to intelligent solution platform.
 A real internal build that turns the Ingenuity Systems website into an interactive product experience rather than a static brochure.
 Traceable scope
 Verifiable evidence
 Challenge What needed to change.
 The original experience described capabilities, but visitors still had to interpret which service fit, what AI could automate, and how to move from interest to a scoped conversation.
 Approach How the system was shaped.
 The site was restructured around business decisions: an LLM/RAG advisor, searchable website knowledge, ROI and maturity tools, an architecture configurator, public project evidence, interactive SVG systems, and a server-side deployment path.
 Outcome What exists now.
 The current platform now has a clearer information architecture, a dedicated engineering portfolio, reusable RAG knowledge generation, secure server-side LLM integration scaffolding, and interactive tools that guide visitors toward a relevant next step.
