 Enterprise Architecture
 Connect intelligence to the systems that actually run the business.
 We design around clear boundaries: experience, orchestration, model/automation services, business systems, data and controls. The exact stack is selected around the job.
 Reference Pattern
 A layered system, not a collection of disconnected features.
 Separating responsibilities makes systems easier to evolve, observe and govern. An AI interface can change without rebuilding the source systems; an integration can be replaced without rewriting the whole product.
