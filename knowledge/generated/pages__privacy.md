 Privacy Policy
 Last updated: September 8, 2026
 This is a practical website privacy-policy starter and should be reviewed for your actual hosting, analytics, CRM, advertising, chatbot and data-processing setup before publication.
 Information we collect
 We may collect information you choose to provide through contact forms, scheduling tools, email, chatbot interactions, or other communications, such as your name, business contact information, company, project details, and messages.
 How we use information
 We use information to respond to inquiries, evaluate project needs, provide services, improve our website and processes, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.
 Service providers
 We may use service providers for website hosting, scheduling, email, analytics, CRM, security, and other business operations. Those providers may process information on our behalf according to their own terms and our agreements with them.
 Cookies and analytics
 If analytics, advertising, or non-essential cookies are enabled, the site should present appropriate notice and consent controls where required by law.
 Data retention and security
 We retain information only as reasonably needed for the purposes described above and use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle.
 Your choices
 You may contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights that may apply in your jurisdiction.
 Where website data goes
 AI Maturity Assessment and solution-finder answers are processed in your browser. The maturity result is saved in browser local storage so it can persist on that device; it is not submitted to our server by the assessment itself.
 Contact-form submissions are sent to the Ingenuity Systems website server, validated, and recorded in a private server-side lead log. When a contact webhook or CRM destination is configured, the same inquiry is also forwarded to that configured business system.
 AI Advisor messages are sent to the website server and then to the configured AI provider so a grounded response can be generated from the Ingenuity Systems knowledge base. The website requests non-persistent model responses; browser conversation history remains in the current page session unless another storage integration is added.
 Optional first-party interaction analytics are only sent after consent. They are recorded in a private server-side event log and may also be forwarded to an analytics webhook if one is configured.
 Contact
 Privacy questions can be sent to info@ingenuitysys.com .
