---
name: Amendment verification
description: The evidence standard for future broad website amendment and consistency passes.
---

Do not mark a broad visual or content amendment checklist complete from markup counts or source searches alone. Verify DOM order, interactive states, accessibility attributes, and representative desktop and mobile renders.

**Why:** A prior source-only sweep showed the requested menu labels and sections but missed broken dropdown behavior, a visually present yet unwired form, and incorrect responsive/section placement.

**How to apply:** For future checklist work, combine static assertions with browser-level interaction checks and screenshots at representative breakpoints before reporting completion.
