# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public Ingenuity Systems marketing site: home, services and four service detail pages, industries and seven industry detail pages, about, careers, contact, work/projects/case studies, public assessment and system-builder tools, and legal/sitemap pages.

## Out of scope
- API/webhook endpoints, archived backups, attached historical assets, screenshots, and generated deliverables not linked as current pages.

## Target audience
- Businesses seeking AI agents, RAG and intelligent systems, workflow automation, custom software, systems integration and data solutions.

## Primary topics
- AI-native solutions, business process automation, software engineering, systems integration, and industry-specific business solutions.

## Rendering and crawler assumptions
- Python standard-library static HTML server with clean URL redirects; page HTML is crawlable without JavaScript, but enhancement-generated content must be checked separately.
- Public pages should be discoverable by search engines, social preview bots and AI retrieval crawlers. No explicit intent to block AI crawling is known.
- Deployment domain is canonical; do not infer a production hostname from development URLs.

- None yet.
