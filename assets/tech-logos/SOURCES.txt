Technology logo assets

Brand marks are local SVG assets so the strip does not depend on external image requests.
Most marks are from Simple Icons 16.31.0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
OpenAI and Salesforce marks are from Font Awesome Free Brands 7.1.0: https://fontawesome.com/search?o=r&m=free
Microsoft Azure uses the existing local Azure icon asset from the earlier site package.
These files are displayed as nominative technology references. Vendor names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
DeepSeek, Google, LangChain, PyTorch and TensorFlow SVGs are local copies from https://cdn.simpleicons.org.
Antigravity, xAI and Manus use user-supplied logos. The Antigravity checkerboard was removed locally; xAI and Manus transparent PNGs were trimmed to their artwork.
